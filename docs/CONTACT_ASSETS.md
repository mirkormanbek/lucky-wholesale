# Contact logos

Telegram and WhatsApp SVGs: Simple Icons repository, icons/telegram.svg and icons/whatsapp.svg (https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons).
MAX SVG: official brandbook https://go.max.ru/brandbook, white icon package https://st.max.ru/brandbook/max-white.zip.

Assets are local in public/; they do not depend on third-party image hosts at runtime. Contact URLs are preserved. MAX is displayed with the provided phone number because a profile URL has not been supplied.

The proposed email address info@luckysport.kz must be provisioned and tested before it is advertised as a working contact channel.
